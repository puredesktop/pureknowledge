# PureKnowledge Design

PureKnowledge captures notes, curates wiki pages, and connects knowledge to suite resources.

The app should feel like a focused knowledge workspace: page editing, package loading, resource-aware organization, and reliable local persistence. Knowledge store, paths, package modeling, workspace hooks, and boot flow remain in the app domain.

Assistant functionality should stay grounded in real knowledge actions: summarizing context, finding pages, creating or updating notes, and connecting knowledge to suite resources through explicit app-owned state changes.
